# Cybersecurity Risk Assessment Toolkit

## Overview

This project demonstrates a basic cybersecurity risk assessment for a fictional small organization.

The project identifies organizational assets, threats, vulnerabilities, likelihood, impact, risk levels, treatment strategies, security controls, and contingency planning considerations.

All information contained in this repository is fictional and was created for educational purposes.

## Risk Assessment Method

Risk is calculated using:

    Risk Score = Likelihood × Impact

Likelihood and impact are rated from 1 to 5.

The educational risk classification used in this project is:

| Score | Classification |
|------:|----------------|
| 20–25 | Critical |
| 12–19 | High |
| 6–11 | Medium |
| 1–5 | Low |

This model is used for demonstration purposes and is not presented as a universal cybersecurity risk standard.

## Project Files

### asset_inventory.csv

Contains the fictional organization's information assets and basic CIA/value ratings.

### risk_assessment.csv

Documents identified assets, threats, vulnerabilities, likelihood, impact, risk scores, risk levels, and treatment strategies.

### risk_calculator.py

Reads the risk assessment and automatically calculates and classifies cybersecurity risks.

### security_recommendations.md

Documents recommended administrative and technical controls for identified risks.

### contingency_plan.md

Provides a basic contingency plan for responding to cybersecurity incidents and restoring critical operations.

## Risk Management Process

The project follows a basic process:

    Identify Assets
          ↓
    Identify Threats
          ↓
    Identify Vulnerabilities
          ↓
    Assess Likelihood
          ↓
    Assess Impact
          ↓
    Calculate Risk
          ↓
    Prioritize Risks
          ↓
    Select Treatment
          ↓
    Recommend Controls
