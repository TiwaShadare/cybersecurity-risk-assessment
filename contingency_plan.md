# Cybersecurity Contingency Plan

## Purpose

This contingency plan establishes basic procedures for responding to cybersecurity incidents that disrupt the fictional organization's information systems.

## Objectives

The objectives are to:

1. Protect people and information.
2. Limit the impact of cybersecurity incidents.
3. Restore critical business operations.
4. Protect the integrity of organizational data.
5. Document and learn from incidents.

## Critical Systems

The organization's highest-priority systems include:

- Customer Database
- Payment System
- Email System
- Cloud Backup
- Network Infrastructure

## Incident Response

### 1. Identification

Identify unusual system behavior, security alerts, unauthorized access, malware, or service interruptions.

### 2. Containment

Isolate affected systems when appropriate to prevent additional damage.

### 3. Notification

Notify appropriate management and technical personnel.

### 4. Investigation

Determine:

- Systems affected
- Information affected
- Likely cause
- Business impact
- Required recovery actions

### 5. Recovery

Restore affected systems from verified clean backups when necessary.

Verify system functionality before returning systems to normal operation.

### 6. Documentation

Document:

- What occurred
- When it occurred
- Systems affected
- Actions taken
- Business impact
- Lessons learned

## Backup Strategy

Critical business information should be backed up regularly.

Backup restoration procedures should be tested periodically.

## Communication

During a significant incident, management should coordinate internal and external communications.

Sensitive incident information should only be shared with authorized individuals.

## Post-Incident Review

After recovery, the organization should review the incident to identify:

- Root causes
- Control weaknesses
- Lessons learned
- Required policy changes
- Additional security controls
