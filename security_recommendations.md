# Security Recommendations

## Overview

The risk assessment identified several risks requiring mitigation. The following controls are recommended for the fictional organization.

## 1. Customer Database

### Risk
Unauthorized disclosure of customer information.

### Recommended Controls
- Implement multi-factor authentication.
- Apply role-based access control.
- Encrypt sensitive information.
- Review user permissions regularly.
- Enable security logging and monitoring.

## 2. Employee Laptops

### Risk
Malware infection caused by unpatched systems.

### Recommended Controls
- Enable automatic security updates.
- Install endpoint protection.
- Restrict administrative privileges.
- Maintain regular backups.
- Provide security awareness training.

## 3. Company Website

### Risk
Exploitation of outdated website components.

### Recommended Controls
- Maintain current software versions.
- Perform vulnerability assessments.
- Remove unnecessary components.
- Monitor security logs.
- Maintain tested backups.

## 4. Email System

### Risk
Employee phishing attacks.

### Recommended Controls
- Implement multi-factor authentication.
- Use email filtering.
- Conduct phishing-awareness training.
- Establish procedures for reporting suspicious messages.

## 5. Wireless Network

### Risk
Unauthorized network access.

### Recommended Controls
- Use strong wireless encryption.
- Change default administrator credentials.
- Maintain current router firmware.
- Separate guest and business networks.

## 6. Cloud Backup

### Risk
Unauthorized access to backup information.

### Recommended Controls
- Require multi-factor authentication.
- Encrypt backup data.
- Restrict access to authorized personnel.
- Test backup restoration periodically.

## Risk Treatment Strategy

The organization should prioritize Critical and High risks before Medium and Low risks.

Available risk treatment approaches include:

- Mitigate
- Avoid
- Transfer
- Accept

Control implementation should consider the organization's risk tolerance, business requirements, cost, and operational impact.
